🚀 Latest Release

The biggest improvements in this version.

Released on

Asprients 2.2.0 introduces a major upgrade to lecture progress tracking. Lecture progress is now calculated directly from actual YouTube playback, synchronized with Firebase, reflected live on the frontend, and automatically marked complete when the lecture is substantially watched.
